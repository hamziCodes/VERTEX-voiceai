PART 1 — Non-Technical Elevator Pitch
VERTEX Voice AI Engine is a universal, modular voice platform and plug-in SDK that lets businesses run natural, low-latency phone and web conversations in 
multiple languages—including seamless English–Urdu switching—without being locked into a single AI vendor. Developers and B2B teams connect it to existing 
apps, websites, phone systems, or APIs through the SDK; non-technical users use a self-serve visual agent builder to create, customize, and deploy voice 
agents on demand. After each call, the platform captures structured outcomes and can route them to CRM and business tools, with compliance controls for 
regulated industries.
