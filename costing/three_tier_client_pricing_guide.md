## 3. Client Proposal & Quotation Guide (How to Price Safely for High Profit)

Always quote the client using a **two-part commercial structure**:
1. **One-Time Setup Fee:** Secures upfront revenue for prompt tuning, knowledge base setup, and phone line configuration.
2. **Monthly Retainer:** Covers ongoing server hosting + 1,000 included voice minutes.
3. **Overage per Minute:** Extra charges if the client exceeds 1,000 minutes.

## 4. Closing Strategy for Your Client

1. **Beating the Competitor:**  
   Pitch **Tier 1** at **$0.05 / minute** or **$0.80 / hour**. It directly beats the competitor’s $0.10/min and $0.98/hour quotes while still yielding you a 40%+ direct margin.
2. **Upselling to Tier 2:**  
   Prepare a quick live demo comparing Tier 1 (Kokoro) and Tier 2 (Cartesia). Once a client hears how natural, lively, and fast Tier 2 sounds, they will almost always upgrade to Tier 2 for the small price difference.
